## Course Description

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to actuarial science and insurance principles. Topics include life insurance, health insurance, property and casualty insurance, risk assessment, mortality tables, premium calculation, and reserving methods. Students will learn to apply quantitative techniques to real-world insurance problems and understand the regulatory framework governing the insurance industry.
